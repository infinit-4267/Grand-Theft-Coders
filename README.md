# 🏙️ Grand-Theft-Coders
## 🤖 NPCs with Free Will: Citizen Behavior Modulation
**Project for Devs of Vice City | Elementos 10.0 Webdev Hackathon** **Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology**

---

### 📖 Overview
In the fictional metropolis of San Viceroy, traditional emergency alerts are no longer effective. Our team, **GrandTheft_Coders**, developed a behavioral modulation engine that transforms "citizens" into "players". By using social influence, gamification, and narrative control, we guide public behavior during city-wide crises, protests, and emergencies while maintaining ethical boundaries.

### 🚀 Problem Statement
Design a system that uses social influence and incentives to guide behavior during:

* **Crises & Emergencies:** Reducing panic and directing safe movement.
* **Misinformation Outbreaks:** Identifying hotspots and stabilizing the narrative.
* **Civic Events:** Rewarding cooperation and positive civic action.

### 🛠️ Key Features

#### 🎮 Civic Quest System
We replaced standard warnings with **Missions**. Instead of "Evacuate the District," citizens receive a quest: *"Secure the Perimeter: Reach the safe zone for 500 Respect Points."* This creates a controlled "illusion of choice" that leads to safer outcomes.

#### 📈 Respect Score (Reputation-Based Access)
A dynamic social metric that rewards pro-social behavior.

* **Incentives:** High scores unlock priority access to city resources and exclusive "Civic Tier" status.
* **Gamification:** Citizens compete to be the most "Civic-Minded," turning safety into a social reward.

#### 🔍 Misinformation Heat Tracker
A real-time analytics layer that identifies areas of high panic or misinformation. The system then deploys **Influencer-Controlled City Alerts** to those specific hotspots to provide verified data and humor-laced de-escalation.
