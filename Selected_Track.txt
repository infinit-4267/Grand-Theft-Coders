NPCs WITH FREE WILL
Problem Statement:
Design a citizen behavior modulation system that uses social influence, incentives, gamification, or narrative control to guide public behavior during city-wide events such as crises, elections, emergencies, or protests.

Expected Outcomes:
- Influence mass behavior positively
- Reduce panic and misinformation
- Reward cooperation and civic action
- Be adaptable to multiple city scenarios
- Respect ethical boundaries while remaining effective

Suggested Product Directions:
- Respect Score System
- Influencer-Controlled City Alerts
- Civic Quest App (missions for citizens)
- Misinformation Heat Tracker
- Reputation-Based Access Control

Unconventional Angle:
- Treat citizens as players, not users
- Replace warnings with missions
- Use humor, memes, or pop culture
- Design controlled "illusion of choice"
